What the seller filter removed — 29 listings across both passes

19 excluded for having a single ad. Kleinanzeigen hides the "N Anzeigen online" counter when a seller has exactly one listing; I confirmed each of these against the seller's own profile page rather than inferring it. Among them were otherwise strong prices — an Avalon VT-737 at €2,450, a Neumann TLM 103 at €480, a DW Fearn VT-1 at €2,800 and a Manley Reference Gold at €5,500 all died here.

10 excluded for having no rating badge. These sellers had 2–7 ads but no satisfaction rating on their profile, meaning no completed buyer feedback. That cost an AKG C414 XLII at €699, a Drawmer 1978 at €720, and a Bricasti M7 at €3,500.

How this was built

  1. Scrape. 36 searches across Kleinanzeigen for compressors, EQs, preamps, effects and mics — generic German terms plus specific brands and model numbers — yielding 696 unique ads.
  2. Filter to category. Dropped non-audio noise that matched on brand names (Aston Martin DBX, Seymour Duncan SSL pickups, Manley engine parts, an "Elysia" cosplay outfit) and out-of-scope audio gear (interfaces, monitors, plugins, monitor controllers). 316 remained.
  3. Split by availability. 114 listings matched a model still sold new; 62 more matched a discontinued or vintage model. The two groups need different benchmarks, so they were priced separately.
  4. Read every ad page for the real asking price, the seller's live ad count, and their rating badges; then read all 145 unique seller profiles to confirm both independently. This also corrected several prices the search-results page had mangled — an SPL Channel One that looked like €2,850 is actually €850, and a Neumann KM 140 pair that parsed as €1,401,850 is €1,850.
  5. Verify new prices at Thomann for stocked lines, and Geizhals for boutique brands Thomann doesn't carry (Manley, Tube-Tech LCA 2B, Chandler, Crane Song).
  6. Verify used prices against comparable units listed on Reverb in euros, discarding parts, manuals and ROM chips, and preferring European sellers and matched conditions.
  7. Re-check the finalists — all 20 confirmed live, none marked reserved, at the prices shown.
Four things to know before you buy. The Tube-Tech is one unit, not three. Seller "Hofmann" has the same LCA 2B posted three times with identical text (ad IDs 3334885020, 3398151902, 3467162047). Listed once here. The Elysia comparison crosses a generation. The listed xpressor 500 is the discontinued original; €929 is its successor, the xpressor neo 500. Treat 53% as indicative rather than exact. Ad count is not a trust score. It filters out throwaway accounts, which is what you asked for, but a rating badge on Kleinanzeigen reflects completed transactions of any kind — it is not a guarantee on a €2,000 channel strip. Prices move and ads sell; verify before travelling. The vintage benchmark is softer than the new-price one. Used comparables are thin — the Drawmer MX30 rests on a single listing, and the SPL Channel One reference is the higher-spec Premium variant, so its 54% is probably flattering. Where a model had no usable comparable at all (Aurora Audio GT4-2, Drawmer LX20, dbx 376, Lexicon MPX500, Schoeps CMC), it was left out rather than guessed at.
